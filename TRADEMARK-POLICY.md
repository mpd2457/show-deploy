logo or branding in a way that suggests the maintainers endorse your product,

Renaming a fork is fine and encouraged. Presenting a fork under this project's
name is not.

## Why there is a separate policy

The code is a general-purpose tool. The name refers to a specific commercial show
business, and the maintainer does not want third parties presenting derivatives
as official. This document is the notice that the mark is not licensed.

Contributors grant copyright and patent rights under that licence; no separate
trademark rights are granted or expected.
